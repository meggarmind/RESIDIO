# Release v2.1.0

**Release date:** 27 October 2026. **Release type:** Minor (new features). Operator steps are necessary: see **Before you upgrade**.

## Summary

Each resident now has one wallet for each charge category. A payment records its purpose and its payer, and an admin can transfer wallet credit. The new **Collections** area records contributions and item purchases that are not debts. A house can have its dues paid by the landlord. Statements, receipts and emails show the estate logo, and WhatsApp logs have their own page.

## New features

### Wallets

- Each resident now has one wallet for each charge category, for example Dues, Development Levy and Transformer Fee. The app spends a wallet only on invoices of the same category.
- A payment now records its purpose (the charge category) and its payer. The app puts the payment credit in the wallet of that category, inside the house of the payment.
- On the payment detail page, an admin can correct the purpose of a payment that was already recorded.
- An admin can use **Transfer credit** to move credit between the wallets of one person. The admin can also move credit to another person who is linked to the same house.
- An admin can reverse a transfer.
- Move-out clearance now checks each charge category on its own. Credit in one category does not cover a debt in another category.
- The levy dry run and the generation check now work for each category. This covers Development Levy and Transformer Fee.

### Collections

- The new **Collections** area records money that is not a debt, such as contributions and item purchases. It is separate from billing.
- An admin can create a collection and record payments against it.
- An admin can name a party to notify about a collection.
- On the bank statement import, an admin can mark a line as a collection payment.
- An admin can split one payment across several collection parts.
- An admin can move a payment between billing and **Collections**.
- A collection payer statement is available from the collection detail page.

### Houses

- On a house, the **Dues paid by landlord** setting sends dues reminders to the landlord. The landlord can pay one amount for the dues of several tenant flats. The app splits the payment across the flats and never gives a flat more than it owes. The receipts show the split.
- The house detail page has a new card that shows each obligation by role and who paid it.
- The house detail page has previous and next arrows to go to the neighbouring houses.
- A house now shows as occupied only when it has a tenant or a resident landlord. A non-resident landlord or a developer does not make a house occupied.
- A house with wallet credit now shows the status Credit in the debt status.

### Billing and invoices

- New invoices have yearly running numbers, for example `INV-2026-000123`. The count starts again at 000001 each year.
- The **Generate Invoices** dialog picks a house by street first and then by house number. It finds a resident by search.
- The **Invoices** list has an Overdue filter. The Overdue card on the billing page opens this filter. It no longer lists invoices that are not yet due.
- The **Invoices** list has a filter bar that shows the active filters as chips. On a phone, the filters open in a sheet.
- The wallet ledger shows monthly invoice debits as one line for each billing item and year.

### Statements

- The account statement groups wallet activity by charge category. It has a **Collections** section.
- The statement shows a run of unpaid monthly invoices as one line. For example, four unpaid Dues invoices for November 2025 to February 2026 become one line. The totals and balances do not change.
- A payment or wallet row ends a run. An invoice that credit pays, in full or in part, stays on its own line.
- WhatsApp statements use this summary form.

### Residents

- The resident **Money** tab has two columns. It shows the payment history in the wallet ledger.
- The wallet ledger scrolls inside its own card.
- In a wallet payment batch, **Select all** selects every invoice. **Select what the wallet covers** selects the invoices that the wallet credit can pay.
- The add resident form is now a wizard with steps.
- When you add a resident from a house, the app sets the house state for you and returns you to the house page.
- The **Residents** table has server paging and sorting. On a phone, it shows cards.

### Payments

- An admin can delete a payment from the payment detail page.

### Estate branding

- The estate logo from **Settings** now shows in emails, account statements, receipts and clearance certificates.
- The logo upload refuses WebP files.
- Statement and receipt PDFs show the estate address from **Settings**.
- Admin receipts and move-out certificates show the estate name from **Settings**.
- Report viewers, report PDFs and scheduled report emails show the estate name from **Settings**.

### WhatsApp

- The new **System > WhatsApp Monitoring** page holds the logs that were in **Settings > WhatsApp**. These are Sent messages, Opt-in registry, Pending contacts, Active sessions, Disclosure log and Clearance certificate copies not sent.
- The page has an **Auto-refresh** control (off, or every 1, 2, 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes) and a **Refresh now** button. Auto-refresh is off by default.
- **Settings > WhatsApp** has a link to the new page. The configuration stays in **Settings > WhatsApp**.
- **Settings > WhatsApp** shows the pilot residents as a table that you can search and filter. The table shows the masked WhatsApp number, the consent state and the reason each resident is in the pilot.
- The Assistant uses the Assistant name from **Settings** in its messages.
- When a resident asks What I owe and has paid everything, the Assistant shows the next payment due. The date counts the wallet credit.
- The Assistant sends the menu as its own message after a final answer.

## Changes

- The sidebar parent items now open their own page. A separate arrow opens and closes the sub-items.
- The page header has its own back control. The top bar no longer shows the greeting twice.
- Tab buttons show a hover state. The active tab is easy to see in dark mode.
- The bank statement import preview has a card layout on phones.
- A page in **Settings** can no longer scroll the whole dashboard when you jump to a section.

## Fixed problems

- The **Invoices** list shows an error block with a **Retry** button when the list does not load.
- The move-out clearance email now has a body. Before, the resident got an empty email.
- Emails that cron jobs send now show the estate details from **Settings**.
- After you add a resident, the house page refreshes. A spinner shows while the app creates the resident.
- The app accepts all valid stored identifiers. Before, a strict identifier check rejected some of them.

## Behind the scenes

- Releases now go through a release gate on the stage to master pull request. The release can also promote the exact stage commit by fast-forward.
- The admin guide has a release notes page for each release.
- The admin design guide is in the repository. Dependencies are updated.
